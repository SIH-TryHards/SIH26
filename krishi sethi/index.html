🏛️ Central & State Government Relief Schemes

Official portals, emergency contact desks, zero-interest credit, and crop insurance claim assistance

Income Support

PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year transferred in 3 installments of ₹2,000 directly to your bank account. Check your status or register on the official portal.

Visit Official Portal 📞 Helpline: 155261 / 011-24300606
Crop Insurance

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Official crop insurance portal. Get coverage against crop loss from drought, flood, or pests. File a claim or check your insurance status here.

Visit Official Portal 📞 Helpline: 14447 / 1800-180-1551
Equipment Subsidy

Direct Benefit Transfer in Agriculture Mechanization

Government portal for farm machinery subsidies and mechanization schemes. Apply online for subsidies on tractors, tillers, and sprayers.

Visit Official Portal 📞 Helpline: State Agriculture Department
Credit & Loans

Kisan Credit Card (KCC) — Low Interest Loan

Get crop loans at just 4% annual interest. In case of natural calamity, banks offer repayment moratorium. Apply at your nearest bank branch.

Visit Official Portal 📞 Helpline: 1800-11-2211 (SBI Kisan)
Irrigation

PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana — Irrigation Subsidy

Up to 55% subsidy for small and marginal farmers to install drip or sprinkler irrigation systems and save water on every crop.

Visit Official Portal 📞 Helpline: State Agriculture Department
Mandi Trading

National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)

Electronic trading portal connecting 1300+ APMC mandis across India. Sell your produce at the best market price without middlemen.

Visit Official Portal 📞 Helpline: 1800-270-0224
